<!-- NOTE: research terms that inform the communication standard without proving it. -->

PURPOSE
- use these terms for further study of two-party communication and uncertainty
- treat the sources as theoretical support, not as proof of the standard

INFORMATION THEORY
- study Shannon entropy and mutual information
- study communication as a reduction of uncertainty between sender and receiver

SET THEORY
- study set intersection as the model for [common ground]
- study set complement and symmetric difference as models for hidden assumptions

CYBERNETICS
- study the Shannon-Weaver model
- study Ashby's law of requisite variety
- study feedback as a method for reducing uncertainty

LOGIC
- study contraposition
- study De Morgan's laws
- study the law of excluded middle

PRAGMATICS
- study Grice's cooperative principle
- study the maxim of quantity
- study conversational implicature and paltering

CONSTRUCTIVE LOGIC
- study constructive and non-constructive proof
- study Curry-Howard correspondence
- study the relation between negation and contradiction
